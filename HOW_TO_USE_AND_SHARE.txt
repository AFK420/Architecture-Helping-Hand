⭐ METHOD 1: SAFARI / CHROME "ADD TO DOCK" (RECOMMENDED — NO WARNINGS!)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the cleanest, Apple-approved way to run on macOS without ANY
Gatekeeper or malware warnings:
  1. Double-click "index.html" to open in Safari (or Chrome).
  2. In Safari: Click the menu bar "File" -> "Add to Dock..."
     (In Chrome: Click the install icon in the address bar -> "Install App").
  3. Click "Add".
  🎉 Architecture Helping Hand is now a 100% genuine, native macOS app
     sitting in your Mac Dock and Launchpad, opening in its own window!

----------------------------------------------------------------------
⭐ METHOD 2: IF macOS BLOCKS THE .APP ("MALWARE / CANNOT BE CHECKED")
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Apple Gatekeeper displays this warning on ALL software downloaded from the
web that does not have a $99/year Apple Developer Certificate.

To bypass this in 5 seconds:

Option A (System Settings — Easiest):
  1. Try opening "Architecture Helping Hand.app" once.
  2. Open Apple Menu () -> "System Settings" -> "Privacy & Security".
  3. Scroll down to the "Security" section.
  4. You will see: "'Architecture Helping Hand' was blocked from use..."
  5. Click the "Open Anyway" button and enter your Mac password or Touch ID.
  macOS will now permanently remember this and open it every time!

Option B (Terminal 1-Line Command):
  1. Open the "Terminal" app on your Mac (press Cmd+Space and type Terminal).
  2. Paste this command and press Enter:
     xattr -cr ~/Downloads/Architecture-Helping-Hand
     (Or type "xattr -cr " with a space, then drag this folder into Terminal).
  3. That's it! All quarantine flags are cleared. Double-click the app to open.

Option C (Right-Click Open):
  1. Hold down the "Control" key (or right-click) on "Architecture Helping Hand.app".
  2. Click "Open" from the menu.
  3. Click "Open" again in the dialog box.
