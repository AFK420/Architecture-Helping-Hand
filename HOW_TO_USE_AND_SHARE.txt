======================================================================
  🏛️ ARCHITECTURE HELPING HAND (v2.8.0) — CROSS-PLATFORM EDITION
       (Full Offline Standalone Support for Windows & macOS)
======================================================================

Thank you for using Architecture Helping Hand! This software runs 100%
locally on your computer without requiring any installation, account,
internet connection, or administrative privileges.

======================================================================
🍎 FOR macOS USERS (MacBook, iMac, Mac Studio, Mac Mini):
======================================================================

Option 1 (Native Mac App — Recommended):
  1. Double-click "Architecture Helping Hand.app" directly in Finder.
  2. The application opens immediately in a borderless window (using
     Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Safari).

Option 2 (1-Click Mac Installer / Setup):
  1. Double-click "Install Mac App.command".
  2. This sets executable permissions, installs the app to your
     ~/Applications folder, and adds a shortcut to your Desktop!

Option 3 (Shell Launcher):
  1. Double-click "Launch Architecture App.command".
  2. It automatically locates your browser and opens the workspace.

Option 4 (Safari / Chrome "Add to Dock"):
  1. Open "index.html" in Safari or Chrome.
  2. In Safari: Click "File" -> "Add to Dock..."
  3. In Chrome: Click the install icon in the address bar -> "Install App".
  4. The app is now a permanent standalone macOS app in your Dock!

======================================================================
🪟 FOR WINDOWS USERS (Windows 10 / Windows 11):
======================================================================

Option 1 (Instant Run):
  - Double-click "Launch Architecture App.bat".
  - Opens immediately in dedicated desktop app mode.

Option 2 (Install Desktop Shortcut):
  - Double-click "Install Desktop Shortcut.bat".
  - Places an "Architecture Helping Hand" icon on your Windows Desktop.

Option 3 (Default Browser):
  - Double-click "index.html".
